The first legally pocketed solid or stripe assigns groups. The eight ball never assigns a group.

Legal contact

Hit your group first. After clearing it, the eight ball becomes your legal target.

03

Fouls

A white ball scratch places the opponent on the baulk line with a forward only shot. Other fouls give normal ball in hand.

04

Winning

Clear your group, then legally pocket the eight ball. An early or foul eight ball loses the frame.

05
