Common questions

Know before you claim.

Do I pay with a debit card or bank transfer?

No. The contract accepts its current claim fee in BNB and your wallet separately pays network gas. Do not make a manual payment to anyone.

Why does the standard allocation stay locked?

The standard path is designed to reward participation. It unlocks over ten milestones as verified gameplay reaches one hundred games.

What is an eligibility proof?

It is a Merkle proof generated from the official eligibility snapshot. It proves your wallet and allocation are included without exposing private wallet information.

Can a claim ask for my private key?

Never. A legitimate wallet connection only requests public account access and clear transaction signatures. Leave immediately if any page requests your secret phrase or private key.
